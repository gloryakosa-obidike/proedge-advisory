About

A builder at the
intersection of law & technology

Glory Akosa-Obidike is a Corporate & Commercial Lawyer, Certified HR Specialist, and Business Advisor with multi-sector experience advising organizations on legal compliance, corporate governance, HR systems, and business structuring.

She has guided startups, SMEs, NGOs, and growing companies through complex regulatory environments, building the legal and operational foundations they need to scale with confidence. A modern, forward-thinking advisor, Glory leverages technology and automation to streamline business processes, strengthen governance, and improve operational efficiency.

She is the founder of ProEdge Advisory, where she helps individuals and businesses navigate legal and structural challenges with clarity and precision. She is also developing the Legal Ease Platform — a system designed to simplify legal processes and bring structure to how legal services are delivered.

Beyond her professional work, Glory is a woman of faith, a mother, and someone deeply committed to building systems, solutions, and a life of purpose and meaning.
